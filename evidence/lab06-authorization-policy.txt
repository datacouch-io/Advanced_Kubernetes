=== AuthorizationPolicy on top of mTLS: identity-based access control ===

AuthorizationPolicy httpbin-allow-fortio-only: ALLOW only from principal
cluster.local/ns/demo/sa/default (i.e. workloads in the demo namespace)

$ from fortio (demo namespace, sa=default, IN the allow list):
Code 200 : 5 (100.0 %)

$ from sleep-other (other-team namespace, sa=default, NOT in the allow list, but still
  fully in-mesh with a valid mTLS certificate):
HTTP 403

Demonstrates: mTLS (PeerAuthentication) proves WHO you are cryptographically;
AuthorizationPolicy decides WHAT that identity is allowed to call. A valid mesh
certificate is necessary but not sufficient -- both layers are independently enforced.
